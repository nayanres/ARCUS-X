Requested-output taxonomy audit (requested-output-v1)
=====================================================

The corrected publication taxonomy uses the z coordinates requested in the
prompt, including the initial state. The complete generated z+1-state
reference and z actions remain unchanged. Live AFS and the historical
classifier retain their original behavior. No inference or API calls were made.

The 13-model non-calibration cohort contains 49,262 completed observations:
39,728 valid and 9,534 invalid. Exactly 3,123 valid responses match the
requested coordinates and are assigned None. All 9,440 full-reference exact
responses are overlong and classified as Horizon Collapse. There are 16,041
corrected Horizon Collapse assignments overall. Of the 13,319 changed labels,
3,123 were Horizon-to-None, 9,440 None-to-Horizon, and 756 other changes.

The modal primary label changes to Horizon Collapse for GPT-5-mini,
MAI-Thinking-1, Kimi K2.5, and Qwen3-235B. Overlong outputs are horizon
signatures, not evidence that the requested transitions were incorrect.
State Tracking remains the modal category for Qwen3 Coder and GLM-5.
The qualitative multi-metric diagnostic finding remains descriptive;
adaptive sampling and taxonomy precedence preclude causal interpretation.

State Tracking has 13,633 explicit assignments and three fallback assignments
(two Kimi and one Ministral-3B). One valid Kimi response (source row 3783,
z=55, tier 3) has an unmapped requested-coordinate divergence. Its mechanism
is not independently established. The 13 synthetic cases test deterministic
classifier consistency, not human or causal validity.

At exact z=20, the 3,604 valid responses comprise 113 None, 1,627 Horizon,
1,186 State, 476 Transition, 150 Semantic, 52 Format, and zero Unknown.
At exact z=30, the corresponding counts among 2,992 valid responses are 77,
1,258, 1,252, 221, 144, 40, and zero.

In the horizon-local analysis, +/-3 has 284 supported cells and +/-5 has
329. State Tracking is uniquely modal in 91/284 and 108/329, respectively;
modal labels agree in 267/284 cells supported at both bandwidths. Neighborhood
membership retains actual horizons; it does not imply exact matching or
task pairing. Coverage uses the pre-established minimum of 10 valid responses,
three valid master seeds and five task hashes. N/A-U means unsampled, N/A-N
means sampled but no valid response, and N/A-I means insufficient coverage.
Where supported, 95% percentile intervals use 2,000 master-seed block
bootstrap replicates, retaining related observations within each seed.

Use requested_taxonomy_model_summary.csv and
requested_taxonomy_model_tier_summary.csv for corrected publication labels,
requested_taxonomy_horizon_taxonomy.csv for corrected exact/local partitions,
requested_taxonomy_precedence_sensitivity.csv for corrected policy sensitivity,
and requested_taxonomy_cross_tab.csv for the validity/length/accuracy
reconciliation. requested_taxonomy_probe_crosswalk.csv and
requested_taxonomy_transition_matrix.csv preserve the comparison with the
historical labels. requested_taxonomy_synthetic_checks.csv records the
injection outcomes. The original analysis_A, analysis_B, reviewer_response,
and horizon_local taxonomy-bearing outputs are historical and are not the
corrected publication taxonomy. Their coverage and non-taxonomy measurements
remain applicable.

Reproduction:
  python audit\correction_2026_11\requested_taxonomy_audit.py
  python audit\correction_2026_11\publish_requested_taxonomy.py
  python audit\correction_2026_11\verify_requested_taxonomy.py

The first command resumes fingerprinted raw-file classification checkpoints.
To regenerate summaries from the existing crosswalk without reclassification,
use requested_taxonomy_audit.py --summarize-existing. The independent
verification checks all 49,262 records, all 1,664 exact/local taxonomy cells,
and reproduces seed-block intervals for one supported cell in each scope.
The unchanged primary metric file and historical probe files remain tracked
without worktree modifications.
