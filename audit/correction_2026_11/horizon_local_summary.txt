Scope and conventions
The 13 publication models are analyzed without inference or modification of
raw logs, scoring, or the primary taxonomy. Calibration anchors are excluded.
Exact horizons are z=20,30. The two separate neighborhood analyses include
only recorded z in [h-3,h+3] and [h-5,h+5], respectively, for h=10,...,150.
Actual horizons and their valid/invalid frequencies are retained in each cell.
The canonical z+1 reference is retained. Conditional Step Accuracy compares
the first z reference coordinates, excluding the initial coordinate, with a
z-1 denominator. Invalid observations never contribute accuracy or taxonomy.

Predeclared support and uncertainty
The uniform minimum for a cell is 10 valid observations, 3 valid master
seeds, and 5 distinct valid recorded experiment hashes. N/A-U means no
observations; N/A-N means observations but no valid responses; N/A-I means
valid responses exist but the minimum is not met. Raw counts and descriptive
means remain available for N/A-I, but intervals and supported comparisons do
not. For a comparison, N/A-U means at least one required cell is unsampled;
N/A-N means no cell is unsampled but at least one has no valid response;
N/A-I means valid data exist on both sides but support or exact-horizon
intersection is insufficient.

For horizon-standardized comparisons, each side independently meets the same
minimum at every retained shared exact horizon; these horizons receive equal
weight. Other horizons are not included in that controlled contrast. Paired
contrasts require matching seed, experiment hash, and gravity; repeats within
a task condition are averaged. A paired result requires the same minimum
number of matched task conditions, seeds, and hashes at each retained
horizon. The experiment hash includes tier; it cannot establish exact
cross-tier task identity. A supplementary check found no valid cross-tier
matches at z=20 or 30 even on the more limited combination of seed, gravity,
grid, initial state, and action sequence.

Intervals use 2,000 percentile bootstrap draws of entire master-seed blocks;
repeated tasks and gravity observations within a seed remain together.
When sides share master seeds, the union of blocks is jointly resampled;
otherwise sides are resampled separately. A standardized contrast receives an
interval only if at least 95% of draws retain both sides at every included
horizon. At only three to five clusters, these intervals can be unstable and
should be interpreted cautiously. Bandwidth differences are descriptive, not
independent replications: neighborhoods can overlap. No significance claim
is based on differing point estimates.

+/-5. Supported same-tier cross-model contrasts: 866/4,680 and 965/4,680;
808 and 886, respectively, also have supported task-condition pairs.

and 55/75 supported +/-5 cases. The direction is descriptive, not causal.

nonzero direction, 39 reverse, and 9 include a zero. The mean absolute change

changed support. For example, Devstral tier 0 minus tier 3 at target 10
changes from -52.0 points (z=10 only) to -18.7 points (equal weights on

windows. At h=100, zero +/-3 but three +/-5 tier contrasts meet the rule.
All 144 supported exact-horizon tier contrasts also meet the rule in both
corresponding neighborhoods; there are no tier contrasts supported only at
the exact z=20,30 under this inclusion rule.

The SQLite caches contain fingerprinted verified raw-file, cell-bootstrap,
and individual comparison checkpoints. Rerunning resumes these checkpoints
only when inputs and relevant code fingerprints match. The script prints
progress for each input file and target. No cache changes original artifacts.

Independent validation
The validator checked 49,328 cohort rows against raw metadata and checked
recorded experiment hashes for conflicting task payloads. It independently
recomputed all 1,664 cells, requested-z scores, taxonomy partitions, inclusion
windows, coverage classifications, common horizons, standardized point
estimates, and task matches for 12,480 comparisons. The unit tests compare the
compressed bootstrap with explicit seed-block resampling.
